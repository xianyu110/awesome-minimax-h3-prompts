## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cinematic lifestyle commercial

SCENE
Red-haired young woman in a white tank top reading "miu miu", loose pink trousers, and sunglasses. A classic yellow convertible (license plate "68J G87"). Palm-tree-lined coastal highway, sandy beach, and ocean.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Low angle tracks the yellow convertible down a sunny, palm-lined street. Cuts to a close-up of the smiling woman driving, hair blowing in the wind.
4-8s — The woman runs away on the beach sand. Cuts back to the moving car where she sits playfully on the rear trunk and then the side door, smiling.
8-12s — Mid-shot of her standing in the moving car with both arms raised. Transitions to a sunset wide shot: she sits relaxed on the parked car's hood on the beach.
12-15s — High-angle drone shot tracks the yellow convertible driving along the coastal highway; the woman stands in the passenger seat with one arm raised toward the ocean.

CAMERA
Smooth dynamic tracking, alternating between low-angle rolling mounts, intimate driver-seat close-ups, and sweeping aerial drone wide shots. Fast-paced, rhythmic cutting.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Sun-drenched natural daylight transitioning to a golden hour sunset. High contrast, warm glow. Vibrant yellow, pastel pink, crisp white, and deep ocean blue.

AUDIO
Rushing wind, classic car engine hum, distant ocean waves. Upbeat, carefree indie-pop instrumental.

AVOID
Static camera, closed-roof modern vehicles, cloudy skies, heavy traffic, muted or desaturated colors.
```
