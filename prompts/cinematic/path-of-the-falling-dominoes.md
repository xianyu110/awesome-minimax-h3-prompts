## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cinematic, surreal architectural transition

SCENE
Black monolithic dominoes, polished marble hall, towering wooden doors, reflective exterior pathway, river, suspension bridges, modern city skyline.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Close-up on the first black domino tipping over, initiating a chain reaction down a long line. Low camera angle on a reflective marble floor inside a grand, dark hall.
4-8s — Camera tracks forward alongside the falling dominoes, rapidly approaching open monumental doors that perfectly frame a distant, sunlit city skyline.
8-12s — The camera pushes directly through the doorway. The interior floor seamlessly transitions into a long, highly reflective exterior structure spanning a wide river, bordered by fog.
12-15s — Continuous forward tracking along the endless line of dominoes. The camera hovers over the elevated path, flanked by massive suspension bridges on both sides, advancing straight toward the glowing metropolis.

CAMERA
Low angle, wide-angle lens. Continuous, smooth forward tracking shot matching the speed of the falling dominoes. Single unbroken take.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Golden hour sunset. High contrast. Warm amber and gold sunlight against deep shadows, dark marble, and silhouetted architecture. Pronounced volumetric sun rays and surface reflections.

AUDIO
Sequential, rhythmic clacking of heavy dominoes falling. Slight interior echo transitioning to sweeping wind ambience. Gradual, swelling orchestral cinematic music.

AVOID
Cuts, camera shake, people, vehicles, on-screen text, harsh midday lighting, visible structural supports beneath the central path.
```
