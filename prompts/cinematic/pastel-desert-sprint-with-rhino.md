## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Surreal high-fashion fantasy, photorealistic 3D

SCENE
A humanoid figure wearing a white knit mask with short bunny ears, an oversized pink floral quilted kimono jacket, mint green patterned trousers, and white platform shoes. Beside them, a large white rhinoceros clad in light-blue, scale-like plated armor covered in prominent gold spikes. The setting is a barren desert with smooth sand dunes under a clear blue sky.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — The masked figure and armored rhino walk forward side-by-side in sync. Medium-wide frontal framing.
4-8s — The duo suddenly sprints rightward across the dunes. A profile tracking shot cuts to an extreme close-up insert of the rhino's spiked armored foot violently kicking up desert sand.
8-12s — Both turn and charge aggressively directly toward the lens. A frontal tracking shot transitions to a low-angle perspective as they pass close to the camera.
12-15s — The subjects run into the distance, then abruptly stop and stand completely still side-by-side, facing the camera while a cloud of dust settles around them.

CAMERA
Smooth tracking and fast panning. Dynamic cuts varying from wide full-body shots to low-angle macro inserts. Steady ground-level stabilization during the running sequences. 

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Harsh midday sunlight creating sharp, high-contrast ground shadows. High-key pastel color palette: baby pink, mint green, powder blue, and metallic gold, set against stark beige sand and a gradient azure sky.

AUDIO
Ambient desert wind, heavy thudding of large hooves, crunching sand, and fast-paced footsteps. Driving, rhythmic electronic percussion track. No dialogue.

AVOID
Modern technology, trees, vegetation, text, watermarks, morphing geometry, motion blur on stationary objects.
```
