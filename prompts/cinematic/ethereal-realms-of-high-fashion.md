## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cinematic surreal fantasy, high-fashion editorial

SCENE
Female subject, elaborate metallic headpiece with circular discs, flowing gowns with long veils. Environments include a cracked white salt flat, a shallow reflective pool with dead silhouetted trees, and a monumental geometric stone stepwell.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — The subject stands on a white desert in a silver gown, cutting to her wearing a teal gown in shallow water, reflecting the twilight sky and dead trees. Wide framing.
4-8s — High-angle wide shot. The subject, now in a green gown, stands on a precipice looking down into a cavernous, smoky stone stepwell.
8-12s — Low-angle shot of the subject in an orange gown walking up stone stairs, cutting to a direct overhead bird's-eye view of her in a blossoming red dress at the center of the geometric steps.
12-15s — Extreme macro close-up of her face. Detailed red iris, dramatic red eye makeup, delicate jeweled forehead adornments, and a single red tear.

CAMERA
Wide-angle lenses for environmental scale. Mix of static wide shots, plunging high angles, low-angle tracking, and top-down perspectives. Match cuts between changing outfits and environments.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High-contrast sunlight on sand and stone. Soft twilight ambient lighting over the water. Directional shafts of light in the stepwell. Color palette shifts dramatically: stark white, burnt orange dunes, midnight teal, emerald green, golden yellow, and vivid crimson.

AUDIO
Ethereal howling wind, gentle water ripples, echoing footsteps on stone, building to a dramatic, tense orchestral string swell.

AVOID
Modern clothing, urban elements, handheld camera shake, shallow depth of field in landscape shots, fast-paced action.
```
