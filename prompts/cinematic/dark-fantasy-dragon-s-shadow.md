## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 1:1 | Style: 1980s dark fantasy cinematic

SCENE
Blonde female warrior in leather armor, male swordsman in a beige tunic, massive horned dragon. Dry grassy valley and a rustic candlelit tavern interior.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — The blonde warrior yells aggressively, striking downward. The man desperately blocks the attack with a glowing blue blade. Framed as a frantic medium close-up.
4-8s — Wide shot reveals a gigantic horned dragon looming behind them. The man falls backward to the ground; the woman stands over him, pointing her sword at his chest.
8-12s — Extreme close-up on the dragon. It opens its jaws unnaturally wide. The camera pushes rapidly down its throat into complete darkness.
12-15s — Cut from black to a dim tavern. The man approaches a green-skinned woman seated at a wooden table with wooden tankards. They smile, lean in, and kiss.

CAMERA
Shaky handheld movement during combat. Dramatic, rapid push-in transition through the monster's mouth. Static medium shot in the final tavern sequence.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Harsh daylight in the field with muted yellows, dusty browns, and glowing red eyes. The tavern uses low-key, warm candlelight casting deep shadows against olive greens.

AUDIO
Metal clashing, aggressive shouts, a deafening monstrous roar, transitioning abruptly to warm tavern ambiance and soft acoustic tavern music.

AVOID
Modern clothing, digital artifacts, visible wires, anachronistic items, contemporary architecture.
```
