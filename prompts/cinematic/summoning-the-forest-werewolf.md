## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Dark fantasy cinematic realism

SCENE
Horned humanoid summoner, crimson robes, skull chain adornments, spiked pauldrons. Giant black-furred werewolf. Setting: Misty pine forest, leaf-strewn dirt floor.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Close-up on the summoner manipulating a conjured magical flame between sharp, black-clawed fingers.
4-8s — Camera pulls back to a wide shot; the summoner stands inside a burning ring of fire, holding the flame aloft.
8-12s — Thick black smoke erupts beside the summoner, rapidly materializing into a massive, muscular bipedal creature.
12-15s — The creature resolves into a giant, snarling werewolf with glowing red eyes, adopting an aggressive stance while the summoner remains still.

CAMERA
Continuous backward tracking shot. Transitions from a tight close-up with shallow depth of field to a wide shot with deep focus. Uninterrupted take.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Low-key diffused forest light. Strong, warm orange and yellow illumination from the fire. Cool teal and dark green shadows. Blood red, charcoal black.

AUDIO
Eerie woodland wind, crackling fire, roaring magical flames. Deep guttural growls and heavy thudding footsteps. Dark ambient drone.

AVOID
Fast camera cuts, bright sunlight, modern elements, cheerful tones, cartoonish features.
```
