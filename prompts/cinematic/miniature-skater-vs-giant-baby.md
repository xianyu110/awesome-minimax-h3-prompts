## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cinematic hyper-realistic miniature action fantasy

SCENE
A miniature man in a white t-shirt and blue jeans skateboarding on a polished hardwood playroom floor among oversized toys, pursued by a giant baby.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — A miniature skateboarder carves away from towering colorful wooden blocks on the bright hardwood floor while a massive hand looms above.
4-8s — The skater glides across the open room, passing a large yellow toy dump truck and furniture as a curious giant baby watches in the background.
8-12s — The skater weaves rapidly between a giant purple ball, plush teddy bears, and toy vehicles while the baby crawls forward aggressively.
12-15s — The baby reaches out and slams a massive hand onto the floor just behind the skater, who narrowly escapes between the baby's limbs.

CAMERA
Low-angle fisheye lens tracking shot at floor level. Smooth, continuous forward camera movement following the skateboarder with kinetic, dynamic action framing.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Bright, natural daylight streaming from bedroom windows. Warm wooden floor tones contrasting with vivid primary toy colors of red, yellow, blue, and purple.

AUDIO
Continuous rumble of skateboard wheels on hardwood floor, playful nursery room ambience, baby giggles and vocalizations, and a heavy thud as the hand strikes the floor.

AVOID
Normal-scale skateboarder, static camera, dark or gloomy lighting, CGI distortion, motion blur, on-screen text.
```
