## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cinematic action fantasy, photorealistic

SCENE
Pale woman in a red, gold, and purple striped jester costume with black blindfold and ruffled collar. Muscular man in black t-shirt, green cargo pants, and white boxing hand-wraps. Modern living room, dark walls, grey rug, blue sofa. Framed wall art reads "VxRender".

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — The jester smiles directly at the camera while the man steps into a fighting stance behind her.
4-8s — The jester executes a rapid spinning high kick, striking the man's guard. Framing tracks the fast motion.
8-12s — The man attempts to strike but is knocked backward onto the rug. The jester lunges downward to finish the fight.
12-15s — The jester stands victorious in the foreground, facing the lens with a wide grin. The man lies defeated in the background.

CAMERA
Dynamic handheld tracking. Medium-wide framing during combat, smoothly panning to follow the martial arts movements. Deep depth of field.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Soft, cool indoor daylight from left windows. Jewel tones of deep purple, crimson, and gold contrast with the room's muted navy, olive, and grey.

AUDIO
Swift fabric swooshes, heavy combat thuds, scuffling shoes on a carpet, driving dark-carnival cinematic music.

AVOID
Slow motion, weapons, taking off the blindfold, shifting room layout, changing text spelling.
```
