## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cinematic gritty thriller action

SCENE
Dilapidated industrial warehouse. Rusty orange metal shelving units, hanging heavy iron chains, concrete floor covered in shattered glass debris. A woman with dark hair tied back, wearing a white button-down shirt heavily stained with fresh blood over the chest, holding a jagged glass shard. A man with dark hair, wearing a distressed, torn black denim jacket over a black shirt.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Wide profile two-shot framed through foreground blurred glass. The woman holds the glass shard defensively, staring down the man across a dusty aisle.
4-8s — Medium action shot. The woman lunges forward aggressively, swinging the glass shard. The man swiftly reacts, intercepting her arm mid-strike.
8-12s — Medium close-up. The man forcefully drives the woman back, pinning her against the rusty metal shelving. Her arm is trapped, neutralizing the weapon.
12-15s — Alternating over-the-shoulder close-ups. First, the woman’s bruised face, blood dripping prominently from her lower lip, lips moving in quiet defiance. Cut to the man’s face, jaw clenched, eyes narrowed with intense anger as he grips her collar.

CAMERA
Handheld dynamic motion, snapping into rapid focus during the struggle. Shallow depth of field isolating faces in close-ups. Fast, kinetic cut rhythm.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Single overhead fluorescent tube casts harsh, directional top-light. Deep shadows filling the background. Cool, sickly cyan and green color grade sharply contrasting with vivid crimson blood.

AUDIO
Industrial hollow room tone, crunching broken glass, sudden fabric rustling, violent scuffle impacts. Dark, pulsing synth drone. Tense, breathless dialogue.

AVOID
Bright key lighting, clean pristine environments, static tripod shots, cheerful atmosphere, slow motion.
```
