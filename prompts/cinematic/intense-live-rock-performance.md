## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 9:16 | Style: Cinematic live music performance

SCENE
Muscular Caucasian man, prominent neck veins, short dark hair. Wardrobe is a long-sleeve black dress shirt, completely unbuttoned to reveal a chiseled, bare chest and abdomen. He holds a vintage silver broadcast microphone on a straight metal stand. Setting is a smoky, dark concert stage.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Medium profile shot. The man grips the microphone stand tightly with one hand, singing aggressively with an open mouth.
4-8s — The camera arcs slowly toward his front. He maintains an intense vocal performance, tilting his head slightly.
8-12s — Angle transitions to a frontal medium close-up. Pronounced neck veins and abdominal shadows are visible as he performs.
12-15s — Tight close-up framing his face and the microphone grill. He strains visibly, mouth wide, delivering a final powerful expression.

CAMERA
Continuous slow, smooth arc and push-in from side profile to a frontal close-up. Shallow depth of field blurring the stage background. No cuts.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Dramatic, high-contrast concert lighting. Warm amber and deep orange spotlights cut through dense stage fog. Strong rim light highlights his profile, jawline, and torso. 

AUDIO
Gritty male rock vocals, heavy electric guitar backing, ambient venue echo. Lips move clearly in sync with loud, sustained singing.

AVOID
Modern microphones, fully buttoned shirts, flat studio lighting, daylight, static camera framing, sudden cuts.
```
