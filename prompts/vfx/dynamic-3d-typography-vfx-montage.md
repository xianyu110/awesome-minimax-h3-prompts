## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 3D typography VFX montage

SCENE
A rapid sequence of diverse 3D environments where thematic words physically manifest from the surrounding elements, spanning cosmic, apocalyptic, digital, and natural landscapes.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Fiery sparks reveal metallic text "MINIMAX H3 TEXT RENDERING". A volcano and falling meteors solidify into molten text "APOCALYPSE". A glowing DNA strand morphs into a running figure, transitioning to bioluminescent text "EVOLUTION".
4-8s — Spacetime distorts around a black hole, shaping the word "SINGULARITY". A swirling fire vortex solidifies into cracked rock reading "INFERNO". Golden neural threads weave together, exploding with light to form "AWAKEN".
8-12s — A white grid room shatters into flying cubes, exposing a red glitching core and the word "CORRUPT". A heavenly gothic cathedral sprouts wings, displaying golden text "ASCENSION". Creeping smoke in an abandoned hospital hallway forms the wispy word "PHANTOM".
12-15s — A dead tree absorbs glowing green energy, blossoming into floral text "REBIRTH". A top-down view of a dense city collapses inward, throwing up debris around heavy metallic text "COLLAPSE", ending in a dust cloud.

CAMERA
Fast-paced montage, dynamic forward zooms, rapid cuts. Wide-angle perspective morphing seamlessly between intense focal points.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High contrast, highly saturated. Distinct palettes per shot: fiery orange, deep space blue, stark white, glowing green, and celestial gold. Strong volumetric rays and lens flares.

AUDIO
Heavy cinematic bass drops, elemental whooshes, digital glitching, ethereal chimes, and building orchestral tension.

AVOID
Misspelled text, flat 2D overlays, static framing, low contrast, soft focus.
```
