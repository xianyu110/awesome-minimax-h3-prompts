## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 9:16 | Style: Photorealistic street fashion video

SCENE
Young Asian woman with long black hair, dynamic transforming wardrobe. Bustling illuminated pedestrian street at night, featuring glowing neon shop signs, vertical banners with Japanese text including "デッソン", overhead streetlamps, and a crowd of varied bystanders.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — The woman walks forward confidently, smiling, wearing a bright yellow button-up jacket, black crop top, dark grey cargo pants, and white sneakers. Framed full body.
4-8s — Seamless transformation. She wears an emerald green satin gown with a thigh-high slit and silver heels, continuing her forward walk.
8-12s — Brief cut to a shocked man in a grey hoodie and woman in a tan trench coat dropping their jaws. Cut back to the main subject, now wearing a bright red trench coat and black boots, passing the astonished onlookers who hold up smartphones.
12-15s — Final seamless transition into a holographic silver zip-up jumpsuit with glowing cyan platform shoes. The background crowd eagerly takes photos.

CAMERA
Eye-level frontal tracking shot, pulling back steadily to keep the walking subject centered. 50mm lens feel with moderate background blur. One fast reaction cutaway. Rhythmic, seamless visual effects editing.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Nighttime urban illumination. Bright ambient streetlamps, vibrant neon accents. Subject outfits feature saturated jewel tones and metallics (yellow, green, red, silver) contrasting heavily with the dimmer, neutral-toned crowd.

AUDIO
Upbeat electronic runway music, ambient street chatter, rhythmic footsteps, camera shutter clicks, background gasps of amazement.

AVOID
Motion blur, anatomical distortions, inconsistent background architecture, muted lighting, static camera movement.
```
