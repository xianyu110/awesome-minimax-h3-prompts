## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 4:5 | Style: Dark fashion editorial music video

SCENE
Two women. First woman: dark bob, black turtleneck, black fur jacket, black leather pants, tall boots. Second woman: blonde hair, black bralette, leather jacket with brown fur collar, black shorts, sheer tights, fur-cuffed boots, kneeling on a vintage brown armchair. Setting: distressed grey walls, brown velvet curtains, ornate floor mirror, patterned rug.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Wide shot of both women posing in the room. A harsh white glowing outline traces the dark-haired woman's body.
4-8s — Rapid digital cuts. Three overlapping, staggered close-ups of the dark-haired woman composite together. Cuts to a medium shot of the blonde woman shifting her pose.
8-12s — Room darkens. Thick, glowing horizontal white energy trails swirl violently around the silhouettes of both women.
12-15s — The energy trails form a circular portal framing the wide shot. Quick cut to an extreme macro shot of black leather moving. Ends returning to the wide shot with dissipating light trails.

CAMERA
Fast-paced music video editing. Static wide framing juxtaposed with jarring digital overlays, multi-exposure clones, and abrupt extreme macro inserts. 

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Low-key, moody studio lighting with heavy shadows. Palette is dominated by charcoal, onyx, deep brown, and black, sharply contrasted by stark, luminous white VFX energy.

AUDIO
Heavy dark synthwave or trap beat. Aggressive digital whooshes, risers, and crackling sound effects synced to the swirling energy trails.

AVOID
Natural sunlight, smooth cinematic panning, slow pacing, pastel colors, acoustic instrumentation, photorealistic documentary style.
```
