## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cinematic hyper-realistic montage

SCENE
Young Asian woman maintaining a steady forward walk and direct eye contact while seamlessly morphing through distinct wardrobes and settings. Elements: corridor, dark blazer, modern office, sheer white gown, fashion runway, spacesuit, spacecraft cupola, racing suit, dark garage.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Woman walks forward in a silver top down a blurred corridor, seamlessly transitioning into a dark blazer within a modern office setting.
4-8s — She morphs into a sheer white couture gown, walking down a runway flanked by silhouette photographers, then transitions to an elevator lobby.
8-12s — She transitions into a white astronaut suit inside a spacecraft cupola, Earth visible through the rear window.
12-15s — She changes into a white, red, and blue racing suit in a garage with a sports car, ending on an extreme close-up of her face.

CAMERA
Steadicam tracking shot moving backward at a constant speed. Subject remains perfectly center-framed. Continuous fluid match-cuts driving the transitions.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Dynamic, adapting per environment. Soft office daylight, bright runway flashes, cool spacecraft LEDs, dramatic high-contrast garage spotlights. Colors shift from warm neutrals to stark whites, cool space blues, and high-impact reds.

AUDIO
Driving electronic bass beat. Subtle ambient sound design matching environments: camera clicks, airlock hums, distant garage echoes. No dialogue.

AVOID
Camera pans, subject looking away, jagged transition cuts, facial distortion, legible logos.
```
