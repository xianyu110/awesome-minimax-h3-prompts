## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cinematic tech commercial with dynamic background transitions

SCENE
A blonde woman wearing large over-ear headphones. Her wardrobe shifts from a dark green top to a black jacket, then a beige ribbed sweater. Settings transition rapidly: a plain white room, a neon futuristic city, a concert arena, deep space, and a mountain peak above clouds.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — The woman smiles in a plain white room, placing headphones over her ears in a medium close-up. As they fit, the background snaps seamlessly to a vibrant neon cyberpunk city.
4-8s — Framed in profile looking right, she smiles. The background morphs from the neon city into a massive, brightly lit concert arena with a large crowd and stage lights.
8-12s — She gazes upwards as the arena dissolves into a dark cosmos featuring a glowing, swirling galaxy. This transitions smoothly into a twilight mountain landscape.
12-15s — Wearing a beige sweater, she closes her eyes and touches the earcups. The background brightens into a sunrise over a sea of clouds. Centered text appears: Hear Beyond Reality

CAMERA
Smooth, continuous framing. Starts face-on, orbits to a profile angle, and pulls back slightly. Match-cut transitions are driven entirely by the environment morphing around the stationary subject.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Soft natural lighting shifts to high-contrast neon pinks and teals, bright white concert spotlights, deep cosmic purples, and warm, golden-hour sunlight.

AUDIO
Quiet room tone snapping to energetic synth-pop, transitioning to a roaring concert crowd, deep ethereal drones, and resolving into an uplifting acoustic melody.

AVOID
Visible brand logos, abrupt camera cuts, poorly lit faces, mismatched eyelines, misspelled text.
```
