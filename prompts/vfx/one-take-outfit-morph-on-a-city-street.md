## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Fashion editorial morphing sequence

SCENE
Female model with a buzz cut and gold hoop earrings, holding various handbags. Busy metropolitan street, red double-decker buses, white delivery van, street lamps, traffic lights, pedestrian extras, shop fronts, metal scaffolding, sidewalk paving stones.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Model walks confidently forward on a crosswalk, wearing a rust-colored suit. The outfit seamlessly morphs into a black silk slip dress with strappy heels. Framed center.
4-8s — Striding along the sidewalk, her clothing transforms into a cream trench coat with matching loafers. It quickly shifts again into a gradient blue-to-yellow denim jacket and jeans with a lime green purse.
8-12s — The outfit changes to a classic blue denim jacket and wide-leg jeans, then morphs into a bright red silk v-neck dress and red heels. A red bus passes behind her.
12-15s — Her attire transforms into a heavy grey tailored coat over black boots. Finally, it morphs into a black leather trench coat. She halts her walk, standing still for the final seconds.

CAMERA
Eye-level focal length. Smooth tracking shot moving continuously backward to match her walking pace. Center-framed full body composition. Single uninterrupted take.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Direct, harsh midday sunlight. High contrast with deep, defined black shadows cast directly onto the pavement. Palette features vibrant reds, bright sky blue, and stark contrast between the changing outfit colors and neutral grey concrete.

AUDIO
Urban street ambience. Rumble of heavy bus engines, faint pedestrian murmurs. Sharp, rhythmic clicking of footsteps. Minimalist, pulsating electronic fashion beat.

AVOID
Cuts, camera shake, inconsistent facial identity, warped architecture, changing environmental lighting conditions.
```
