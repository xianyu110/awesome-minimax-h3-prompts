## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 4:3 | Style: Cinematic match-cut transformation sequence, hyper-realistic

SCENE
Young woman, braided crown hairstyle, hazel eyes. Transforming wardrobe: business suit, cyberpunk bodysuit, explorer jumpsuit, winter parka, space armor. Transforming settings: tropical beach, neon city, jungle ruins, snowy mountains, deep space.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Woman in black suit walks forward on tropical beach at sunset, seagulls flying; framed as medium close-up pulling back.
4-8s — Setting morphs to rainy cyberpunk city with flying cars; wardrobe becomes black and silver sci-fi bodysuit; medium shot, continuous forward walking.
8-12s — Background shifts to jungle temple ruins with waterfalls and macaws, outfit becomes tan explorer jumpsuit; then morphs to snowy mountains and white winter parka.
12-15s — Environment dissolves into deep space with planets; outfit becomes white glowing space armor, stepping onto a crystal hexagonal platform; wide medium shot.

CAMERA
Steady tracking shot, reverse dolly moving backward at constant walking pace, subject centered. Seamless morphing transitions, no cuts.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Dynamic environmental lighting. Warm golden hour (orange, cyan), wet neon (magenta, blue), soft jungle daylight (emerald, tan), stark space glow (purple, white).

AUDIO
Rhythmic electronic beat, rhythmic footsteps, waves, city hum, bird calls, icy wind, ethereal synth drone.

AVOID
Camera cuts, facial feature changes, breaking eye contact, jerky movements, inconsistent walking pace.
```
