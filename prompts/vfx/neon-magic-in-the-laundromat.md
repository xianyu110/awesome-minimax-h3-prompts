## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: First-person mixed-media magical realism blending live-action with glowing neon 2D animations.

SCENE
Unseen protagonist's hands, brown jacket sleeve. Retro laundromat with metallic washing machines, beige tiles, fluorescent lights, wooden bench, white laundry basket. Floating neon line-art animations: jellyfish, sock, fish, toast, truck, geometric energy.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Fingers pinch a fuzzy, glowing purple orb. Cut to first-person view looking down into a laundry basket where a neon purple line-art jellyfish floats above white garments.
4-8s — Low angle pan to a glowing neon sock hovering beside a real grey sock on the floor. Cut to first-person hand reaching toward a washing machine door; a neon cyan fish swims on the glass.
8-12s — Hand opens the washing machine. Neon purple toast and a white truck float out amid drawn smoke. Cut to hand holding a glowing purple geometric cube near a blue vending machine.
12-15s — Wide shot of the laundromat. Neon pink and cyan energy spikes erupt across the floor from the central machines. The entire room suddenly transforms into a neon line-art blueprint floating in a dark, starry cosmic void.

CAMERA
First-person perspective. Handheld movement. Shallow depth of field with macro focus on glowing objects. Fast-paced rhythmic cuts. Concludes with a wide, static establishing shot.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Harsh overhead fluorescent practical lighting. Physical space features muted beige, silver, and white. Animated elements emit intense luminescent neon purple, cyan, and pink. Final scene is deep space-black with vibrant neon outlines.

AUDIO
Humming fluorescent lights, tumbling washing machines, synthetic magical chimes, electronic synth beat.

AVOID
Third-person perspective, photorealistic CGI entities, natural daylight, static framing, deep depth of field.
```
