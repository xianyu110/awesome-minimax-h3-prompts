## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 2D animated cartoon, vibrant flat colors, thick outlines, comic book style

SCENE
Red-haired man with a scowling expression. He wears a yellow puffer jacket with a green fur hood and smiley face patches, blue jeans, and mismatched high-top sneakers (one pink/purple, one yellow/green). Setting: stylized city street with brightly colored buildings, clear blue sky, puffy white clouds, and a distant clock tower.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Skater rides down the street, trailing stylized blue and yellow flames from the skateboard wheels. Framed as a low-angle tracking shot beside him.
4-8s — Skater continues riding past colorful storefronts, including a green shop with a "BAD OZA" sign. The camera tracks slightly ahead.
8-12s — Extreme close-up on the mismatched sneakers on the skateboard deck. Thick blue and yellow flames erupt dramatically from the wheels.
12-15s — Cut to a high-angle tracking shot behind the skater, following him down the center of a dashed road toward the clock tower.

CAMERA
Dynamic tracking movement. Utilizes low-angle profile shots, cuts to an extreme close-up, and finishes with a trailing shot. Smooth, continuous forward momentum.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Flat, bright daytime illumination. Highly saturated color palette. Dominant bold colors: mustard yellow, cyan, vibrant red, emerald green, and neon pink.

AUDIO
Rolling skateboard wheels on asphalt, rushing wind, and the crackling whoosh of fire. Fast-paced punk-rock instrumental music. 

AVOID
Photorealism, 3D rendering, CGI shading, muted tones, static camera, soft edges, realistic proportions.
```
