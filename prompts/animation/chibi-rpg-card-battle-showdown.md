## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 3D anime chibi fantasy RPG video game

SCENE
Floating circular stone arena above clouds. UI overlays including health bars, a "TURN 1" banner, and an "END TURN" button. Six chibi anime characters in animal kigurumi costumes stand on glowing hexagonal tiles.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Static wide shot of the arena. Three stylized playing cards slide into view at the bottom edge.
4-8s — The center card rises. A massive, glowing golden spirit beast manifests over the left-side characters.
8-12s — A glowing red and white shield forms in front of the right-side lion character. Floating "-120" text pops up above the right team amidst rising ground mist.
12-15s — A pink magical slash impacts the right team, generating "-128" and "-150" text. A glowing torii gate appears. The top banner updates to "TURN 2" and right-side health bars deplete.

CAMERA
Fixed high-angle wide shot, imitating a static video game perspective. Zero camera movement or cuts.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Bright daytime sunlight, soft volumetric clouds. Cool stone grays, bright cyan and neon red UI accents, gold and magenta magical VFX.

AUDIO
Upbeat fantasy orchestral music. UI card-slide clicks, magical conjuration chimes, and energetic impact sound effects during the attack.

AVOID
Photorealism, live-action, shaky camera, dramatic shadows, dynamic camera tracking, realistic human proportions.
```
