## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Surreal fantasy animation blending stylized 3D environments with 2D character illustration

SCENE
A short-haired traveler in black clothing explores a mystical alien jungle filled with giant trumpet fungi, translucent glowing pink mushrooms, surreal hybrid creatures, and whimsical 2D spirits.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Wide tracking shot of the traveler walking through a lush fungal forest, cutting to a medium shot as they pluck and eat a piece of a soft, translucent pink mushroom.
4-8s — Macro close-up on the traveler's wide hazel eyes dappled in light caustics, cutting to wide blooming mushrooms releasing pink vapor, then tracking a living one-eyed porcelain teapot creature walking on bird feet.
8-12s — Medium shot of a moss-backed hippo-snail hybrid blinking slowly, transitioning to three flat 2D cartoon creatures (a one-eyed quadruped, a round pink blob, and a green seedling) scurrying across a mossy log.
12-15s — Over-the-shoulder view of the traveler watching the floating 2D spirits among massive banyan roots, cutting to a black screen displaying the white logo and text "路西 STUDIO LUCY.D.XIE".

CAMERA
Smooth side-scrolling tracking, tight macro focal depth for facial close-up, static character framing, rhythmic montage cuts.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Diffused forest canopy light, soft dappled caustics, bioluminescent pink glow. Palette of moss green, coral red, blossom pink, earthy brown, and porcelain blue.

AUDIO
Rustling leaves, soft squelching bite, ethereal shimmering chime, whimsical animated creature squeaks, silent final cut.

AVOID
Photorealism, urban elements, modern machinery, harsh neon lighting, rapid shaky camera.
```
