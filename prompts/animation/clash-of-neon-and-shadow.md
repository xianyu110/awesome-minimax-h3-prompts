## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 3D anime cel-shaded animation, high-octane action sequence

SCENE
Blonde female warrior in a bright orange military trench coat and black boots. Hooded antagonist in a tattered grey cloak. Desolate concrete platform against an industrial overpass under a clear blue sky.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Panning across empty concrete to reveal the warrior clashing a glowing green energy blade against the antagonist's black smoke weapon in a fast-paced medium shot.
4-8s — The antagonist unleashes a linear blast of black energy. Framed wide as the warrior steps back, cutting to a high top-down aerial perspective of the combatants circling.
8-12s — Over-the-shoulder view behind the antagonist wielding a solid black sword. Cuts to a low, tilted angle as the hooded figure swings the dark blade with intense motion blur.
12-15s — Wide shot. The warrior counterattacks with a massive, sweeping neon green energy arc that impacts the ground and the antagonist, kicking up a thick cloud of dust.

CAMERA
Dynamic sweeping movements. Rapid cuts between extreme low angles, over-the-shoulder views, and top-down tracking shots. Pronounced motion blur on weapon swings.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Bright, high-contrast sunlight. Vibrant orange and neon green contrasting sharply with muted greys, stark black energy shadows, and a flat blue sky. 

AUDIO
Wind ambience, sharp metallic clashes, deep bass whooshes for magical energy attacks, rumbling impact debris. High-tempo, aggressive action score.

AVOID
Photorealism, slow pacing, static camera, soft focus, text, watermarks.
```
