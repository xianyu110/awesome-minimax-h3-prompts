## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Mixed media, live-action with 2D anime character integration

SCENE
Car interior. Passenger: 2D anime girl, split pink/blue bob hair, white collared shirt, blue skirt, unamused expression. Driver: Live-action man, green afro, oversized red/blue 3D glasses, patterned navy suit, pink bowtie, goatee. Setting: Black leather car seats, seatbelts, passing suburban streets outside.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Dashboard POV wide shot. The driver speaks energetically, holding the steering wheel. The anime passenger sits motionless.
4-8s — Close-up of the anime girl. She rests her chin on her hand, displaying a profoundly bored, deadpan expression with half-closed eyes.
8-12s — Close-up of the driver talking animatedly and pumping his fist. Cut to a wide two-shot showing the girl's unimpressed profile in the foreground while the driver steers in the background.
12-15s — Close-up of the driver explaining something. He gestures with his right hand, then awkwardly rubs the back of his neck. A blue backpack is visible behind him.

CAMERA
Dashboard-mounted static wide angles alternating with tight facial close-ups. Sharp, rhythmic cuts highlighting the contrast in character energy.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Diffused, overcast daylight from windows. Saturated contrasting colors: neon green hair, bright cyan/magenta glasses, pastel pink/blue anime hair, deep navy suit with yellow/red patterns.

AUDIO
Muffled engine hum and road noise. Upbeat, comedic backing track. Enthusiastic male dialogue syncing with the driver's lips.

AVOID
Lighting mismatches between 2D and live-action elements, motion blur on the anime character, shifting car interior layouts, camera shake.
```
