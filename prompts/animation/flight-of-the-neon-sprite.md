## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 9:16 | Style: 3D animated sci-fi fantasy

SCENE
Small, furry red creature with a feline face, glowing cyan horns, and translucent cyan wings. Futuristic nocturnal city featuring sweeping, curved metallic skyscrapers. Calm, mirror-like waterway. Starry night sky displaying a vibrant Milky Way.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Creature speeds past sleek curved buildings; framed in tight profile with edge motion blur.
4-8s — Creature ascends steeply toward a towering, illuminated skyscraper; framed from a low rear angle highlighting its glowing cyan spine.
8-12s — Creature glides horizontally over glassy water; framed directly from behind, tracking its flight toward distant city arches.
12-15s — Transitions from a wide lateral tracking shot past giant structural rings to a hovering, front-facing close-up of the creature smiling directly at the lens.

CAMERA
Dynamic drone tracking. Fast fluid movements shifting from tight profiles to wide rear-follow shots. Wide-angle lens with deep depth of field. Continuous single-take motion.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Nighttime ambient starlight paired with neon city glow. Deep indigo sky, bright luminescent cyan, warm golden-yellow interior building lights, vibrant ruby-red fur. Polished specular reflections on water and metallic architecture.

AUDIO
Wind rushing. Soft magical chiming synchronized with wing flaps. Serene, wondrous orchestral synth music. Low ambient hum of a futuristic metropolis.

AVOID
Daylight, jagged architecture, human figures, typography, realistic animals, static camera, muted colors.
```
