## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Voxel-based 3D animation, blocky pixel art aesthetic with realistic lighting

SCENE
First-person character with blocky, pixelated arms. A forest campsite beside a flowing stream. Voxel-style trees, grass blocks, dirt paths, gray stones forming a campfire ring, and a circular tree stump prep station.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Camera pans across the stream to the tree stump. A blocky hand holds an iron cleaver, chopping voxel ingredients into smaller yellow, orange, and brown cubes.
4-8s — Perspective shifts to the campfire ring. A dark, blocky frying pan rests on stones, holding the chopped cubes. Orange flames ignite beneath the logs, generating rising gray smoke.
8-12s — A square glass shaker appears on a rock. The first-person arm grabs the shaker and tips it over the pan. Black pixelated seasoning falls onto the cooking food cubes.
12-15s — Large brown voxel meat blocks are added to the pan. Two blocky hands present the completed meal—stacked meat blocks, yellow cubes, and a red tomato block—served on a scalloped wooden plate held above the roaring fire.

CAMERA
First-person POV throughout. Fluid, continuous movement mirroring head and hand motions. Close-up framing focused entirely on the culinary tasks and ingredients.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Natural sunlight filtering through the forest canopy, creating sharp shadows. Dynamic warm orange illumination from the campfire. Earthy palette of forest greens, dirt browns, charcoal gray, and bright yellow-orange food tones.

AUDIO
Ambient forest wind and trickling stream water. Rhythmic heavy thuds of a chopping cleaver. Intense crackling of burning logs, loud sizzling of frying food, and the sharp rattle of a seasoning shaker.

AVOID
Smooth organic geometry, high-polygon meshes, realistic human anatomy, modern kitchenware, visible faces.
```
