## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 3D stylized chibi animation, monochromatic cinematic realism.

SCENE
Rainy Parisian street at night. A covered bus stop with a wooden bench. Two stylized chibi characters: a woman with a black bob in a white trench coat, and a man with slicked hair in a black double-breasted overcoat.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Title text over a blurred rainy street: "アンリとコレットの 愛のドラジェ HENRI ET COLETTE Les Dragées de l'amour". Cuts to a wide shot of the man and woman sitting apart on the bench.
4-8s — Medium close-up of the woman looking down, then looking up. Cuts to an over-the-shoulder shot of the man reacting. 
8-12s — Close-up of the woman smiling. Returns to the wide shot of both characters sitting together on the bench.
12-15s — Sequence of three black title cards with white text. First: "アンリとコレットの 愛のドラジェ HENRI ET COLETTE Les Dragées de l'amour". Second: "ep.1「傘のない恋人」 Les Amants sans parapluie". Third: "ててつろう × Hailuo".

CAMERA
Static camera, shallow depth of field, standard shot-reverse-shot dialogue cut rhythm.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Low-key atmospheric lighting, soft ambient streetlamp glow. Desaturated cool palette, dominated by black, white, and silver-gray.

AUDIO
Heavy rain ambience, melancholic piano music. 

AVOID
Photorealism, vivid colors, dynamic camera movement, cluttered backgrounds.
```
