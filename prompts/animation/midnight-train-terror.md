## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 21:9 | Style: 2D anime, dark supernatural thriller

SCENE
Man in grey suit and tie with briefcase. Pale woman with long black hair in school uniform. Nighttime train platform, train interior with green seats, dark grassy embankment.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Man looks at phone on empty platform. Woman stands facing the tracks. Train arrives with blinding headlights, blowing her hair.
4-8s — Doors open. Cut to interior. Man and woman sit quietly on opposite benches in an empty carriage.
8-12s — Man leans forward to touch her knee. She smiles with pale, sunken eyes. Sudden cut to chaos: panicking passengers flee toward the camera as a dark, shadowy entity attacks.
12-15s — Man crashes backward through the train window, shattering glass. He tumbles down a grassy embankment next to the moving train. Bruised in the grass, he looks up as the woman crawls toward him, smiling ominously.

CAMERA
Static mid-shots and low angles build tension initially. Abrupt switch to erratic, fast-paced cuts and shaky handheld movement during the supernatural attack and fall.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Harsh fluorescent station lights, blinding white train headlights. Desaturated cool palette: teal, concrete grey, muted greens, deep blacks. Bright orange sparks during the fall.

AUDIO
Low ambient hum, loud rushing wind, screeching train brakes, shattering glass, chaotic screams. Ominous, low-frequency droning music.

AVOID
Daylight, warm color grading, 3D rendering, realistic textures, slow-motion action, cheerful expressions.
```
