## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Claymation stop-motion animation

SCENE
Anthropomorphic sheep, smooth black clay face and limbs, textured white wool body, wide eyes, purple eyelids. Settings include thick green plant stalks, a dark interior tunnel, and a refuse pile featuring rusty tin cans, torn newspaper, and a large green rubber boot.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Close profile. The sheep pushes its head and front arm between thick green stalks, peering into a glowing hollow space.
4-8s — Low frontal angle. The sheep crawls forward on its belly through a dark environment. A glowing blue mushroom briefly illuminates the background behind it.
8-12s — Medium shot. The sheep slumps dejectedly into a pile of garbage, shoulders dropped, resting against cans and a green boot.
12-15s — The sheep suddenly sits up straight, eyes wide, lifting both front arms in a startled, alert posture.

CAMERA
Macro lens perspective, shallow depth of field. Low, eye-level angles. Quick straight cuts between the stalk entrance, tunnel interior, and trash pile. Simulated stop-motion frame rate.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High contrast dynamic lighting. Warm golden directional glow inside the hollow tunnel. Bright neon blue accent lighting from the mushroom. Color palette: stark black, off-white, vibrant green, warm rust, charcoal.

AUDIO
Rustling thick foliage, sliding friction on dirt. Dull thuds, metallic clanks, and paper crinkles. Plucky, curious pizzicato string music. No dialogue.

AVOID
Photorealism, flat 2D animation, smooth 60fps CGI motion, human subjects, clean modern environments.
```
