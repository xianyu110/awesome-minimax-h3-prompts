## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 3D animated cinematic action anime

SCENE
Two superpowered martial artists. Fighter One wears an open white shirt and radiates a fiery golden aura. Fighter Two wears a black leather jacket and dog tags. Environments include a desolate city street and a crumbling stadium.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Fighter One charges up, emitting bright golden light. A close-up reveals cracked skin with glowing blue energy and luminous eyes. Fighter Two stands defiantly in a sunset-lit street, then charges aggressively toward the camera.
4-8s — Fighter One delivers a sweeping, flame-wreathed punch, knocking Fighter Two backward. A tight close-up focuses on Fighter One's blazing yellow eyes. Cut to an expansive aerial shot of the two figures confronting each other in the center of a heavily damaged stadium.
8-12s — Glowing blue electricity concentrates around a forearm. The arena floor detonates, sending massive chunks of concrete and dust flying upward into the stadium sunlight.
12-15s — Fighter Two roars in slow motion. A fist surging with blue lightning punches directly toward his face. Fighter One stands enveloped in golden energy, ending on a final piercing stare.

CAMERA
Rapid action cuts. Alternates between intense extreme close-ups, dynamic tracking during sprints, and sweeping high-angle wide shots. 

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High-contrast dramatic lighting. Dominant warm tones of amber, gold, and sunset orange, sharply contrasted by bright cyan and blue electrical effects. Deep black shadows.

AUDIO
Low rumbling bass, electric crackling, heavy impact thuds, shattering concrete, and aggressive yelling. Intense electronic action score.

AVOID
Watermarks, on-screen text, cel-shading, slow pacing, static camerawork.
```
