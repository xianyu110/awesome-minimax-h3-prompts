## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 2D anime animation, high-octane action

SCENE
Female anime warrior, deep magenta hair in a high ponytail, glowing pink eyes, black tactical bodysuit, silver shoulder armor, dark thigh-high boots, wielding a massive curved katana. Setting is a barren, desolate rocky wasteland under heavy dark storm clouds.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Extreme wide shot of the tiny figure in a dark, rocky valley. Quick cut to an intense, dynamic close-up as she readies her katana. Bright blue lightning arcs violently across the frame.
4-8s — Low-angle perspective looking directly up the broad, metallic sword blade. She leaps through the air, surrounded by heavy, glowing blue energy trails and motion blur.
8-12s — Mid-air framing against a swirling, vortex-like cloudy sky. Extreme close-up of her face showing fierce determination, lips parted in a shout, eyes glowing.
12-15s — Full body shot floating high in the dark sky, raising her sword to gather power. A massive, screen-filling vertical pillar of blue lightning strikes the rocky ground below.

CAMERA
Dynamic anime combat framing. Extreme low angles, Dutch tilts, and extreme close-ups. Fast cut rhythm, high-speed tracking, forced perspective highlighting the sword.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Dark, moody atmospheric lighting. High-contrast neon illumination driven by magical effects. Midnight blues, charcoal greys, deep magenta, and blinding electric cyan.

AUDIO
Howling wind ambiance, sharp metallic blade swooshes, heavy electrical crackling, massive explosive bass drop. Fast-paced, driving electronic-orchestral battle music. Wordless animated battle cry.

AVOID
Photorealism, live-action humans, bright daylight, modern urban settings, slow pacing, static camera.
```
