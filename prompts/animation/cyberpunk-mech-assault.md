## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 2D anime animation, sci-fi cyberpunk action

SCENE
Giant green combat mech featuring orange safety stripes and "02" painted on the chest plate. Cyborg woman with flowing black hair, oversized orange jacket, white crop top, beige cargo pants, metallic arm, and robotic leg. Ruined city street filled with concrete rubble and abandoned cars.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — The heavy mech steps forward through the urban debris, carrying a massive black assault rifle. Framed at a low angle.
4-8s — The mech raises the rifle and fires, generating a bright yellow muzzle flash. Framed as a medium wide shot.
8-12s — The cyborg woman sprints directly toward the lens. A massive fiery explosion erupts in the background behind her. Tracking shot.
12-15s — The woman leaps high into the air and violently punches the mech's sensor head. A bright impact flash occurs, followed instantly by shattering metal and flying debris. Dynamic mid-air framing.

CAMERA
Low-angle perspective emphasizing scale. Tracking camera pulls backward during the sprint sequence. Fast cuts. Dynamic action framing with exaggerated perspective.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Dusk lighting under a cloudy evening sky. Dominant olive green, warm orange, and muted concrete grey. High contrast points illuminated by bright yellow fire and explosive energy.

AUDIO
Heavy mechanical stomping, rapid automatic gunfire, deafening explosive blasts, metal shattering. Upbeat, fast-paced electronic rock music.

AVOID
Photorealism, static framing, clean pristine environments, completely biological human anatomy, slow motion pacing, real-world branding.
```
