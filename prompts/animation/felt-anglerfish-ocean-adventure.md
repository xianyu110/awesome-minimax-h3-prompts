## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 3D needle-felt stop-motion animation, diorama

SCENE
Round orange plush anglerfish with blue spots, large black eyes, and a glowing head-lure. Felt-textured underwater environments featuring vibrant corals, a wooden shipwreck, glowing crystals, and volcanic vents.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — The orange felt fish swims rightward across a bright coral reef. A green turtle and tiny blue fish swim below in the sunlit water.
4-8s — The fish navigates a wooden shipwreck alongside a pink octopus and grey seal, then enters a dark rocky cave illuminated by glowing crystals and bioluminescent squid.
8-12s — Exiting the cave, the fish encounters a striped fish, then swims over glowing orange magma vents. Small crabs crawl below while manta rays glide above.
12-15s — After passing a yellow spiked pufferfish near the vents, the orange fish enters a sunny kelp forest. It turns to face the camera directly, centered symmetrically as bubbles rise.

CAMERA
Smooth, continuous tracking following the fish's journey. Slow, steady horizontal pans. Concludes with a static, centered close-up.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Top-down volumetric sun rays, soft bioluminescent point lights, and warm under-lighting from magma. Deep oceanic blues, vibrant coral pinks, fiery oranges.

AUDIO
Subtle underwater bubbling, muffled water flow ambience, whimsical instrumental background music.

AVOID
Photorealism, sharp CGI edges, human characters, camera shake, text.
```
