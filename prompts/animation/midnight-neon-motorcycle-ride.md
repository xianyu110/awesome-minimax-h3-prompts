Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Angular stylized 2D animation with sharp geometric shapes and vibrant cel-shaded rendering

SCENE
An urban night setting featuring a young man with a white cheek bandage, oversized black jacket, and light cream trousers on a white cafe racer motorcycle. SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Medium wide shot outside a fluorescent-lit storefront; the man straddles his motorcycle lighting a cigarette as the woman steps out the glass door and gracefully mounts the back seat.
4-8s — Medium tracking shot inside a subterranean expressway tunnel; the woman leans forward, resting her chin and arm affectionately on his shoulder while her dark hair blows backward in the wind.
8-12s — Close-up side tracking profile; the man steers calmly with a glowing cigarette between his lips while the woman peacefully closes her eyes against his jacket, cyan ceiling lights flashing rapidly overhead.
12-15s — Low-angle perspective from behind the motorcycle as it accelerates down the center line of the roadway, roaring toward the tunnel's vanishing point under repeating illuminated overhead fixtures.

CAMERA
Cuts from a static street-level medium shot to fluid side-tracking profiles matching the motorcycle's velocity, concluding on a low-angle trailing shot with deep perspective.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High-contrast nocturnal aesthetics dominated by deep navy shadows, bright warm convenience store glows, and intense teal and cyan tunnel strip lights casting dramatic edge highlights.

AUDIO
Subdued ambient city murmur transitioning into a guttural, throaty four-stroke motorcycle engine revving, rushing atmospheric wind effects, and a moody electronic synthwave beat.

AVOID
