## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Mixed media, photorealistic live-action blended with glowing 2D vector animation

SCENE
Human hands, rustic wood kitchen counter, stainless steel sink, ceramic mug, metal spoon, striped towel, window. Overlaid with glowing 2D animated flora and fauna.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Open palm holds a glowing animated green sprout. Camera pans right along the sunlit counter to a neon green animated caterpillar crawling near a spoon and mug.
4-8s — Animated green paper plane arcs from a glass bottle to the mug. Cut to a hand reaching toward an animated orange goldfish floating mid-air over a dark sink.
8-12s — Panning past a folded yellow-striped towel, revealing an animated green-and-pink snail, followed by a glowing orange animated circle resting on the fabric.
12-15s — Camera faces a window framing a sunset landscape, heavily overlaid with glowing animated orange flowers. A hand enters the foreground, pinching a bright animated green leaf.

CAMERA
Intimate macro close-ups, smooth lateral panning, organic handheld motion, shallow depth of field.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Warm golden hour sunlight, sharp diagonal window shadows. Earthy browns and warm whites contrasted against neon green, bright orange, and glowing pink animations.

AUDIO
Quiet room tone, soft magical synth chimes for the animated elements, mellow acoustic guitar melody.

AVOID
Photorealistic CGI creatures, fully animated backgrounds, flat lighting, sterile environments, erratic fast cuts.
```
