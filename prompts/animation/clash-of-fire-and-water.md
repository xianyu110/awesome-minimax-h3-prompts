## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 3D fantasy CGI animation, cinematic video game trailer

SCENE
Male warrior in a blue tunic and silver chainmail, and a female warrior with flowing pink hair in red and silver armor. Ancient stone courtyard with mossy cobblestones and traditional temple architecture.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — The warriors engage in martial arts combat, generating sweeping trails of translucent blue water and orange fire from their arms in a medium two-shot.
4-8s — They jump back into wide stances, gathering magical energy. Framed as a wide shot capturing the temple courtyard.
8-12s — They project massive elemental avatars: a large serpentine blue water dragon and a glowing fiery phoenix. The creatures fully materialize and rush toward the center.
12-15s — The avatars collide in a blinding flash. A massive shockwave of thick gray smoke and airborne stone debris obscures the scene, pushing the bracing characters to the edges of the frame.

CAMERA
Eye-level perspective, steady wide framing. Subtle pull-backs to accommodate the massive elemental creatures. Locked-off spatial axis focusing on the central clash.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Volumetric sunlight rays piercing down through the temple roofs. High-contrast, vibrant palette: neon blues, fiery oranges, and bright pinks against muted moss greens and stony grays.

AUDIO
Martial arts whooshes, splashing water, roaring fire, high-pitched screeches, heavy bass explosion, crumbling stone, driving orchestral battle music.

AVOID
Text, watermarks, modern clothing, photorealism, shaky camera, modern architecture.
```
