## Prompt

Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 21:9 | Style: 2D anime-style animation, cyberpunk aesthetic

SCENE
Young woman, black high ponytail, oversized orange puffer jacket, black crop top, crossbody bag, facial markings. Young man, dark hair, black tactical suit with red and blue accents. Futuristic neon city streets, curved cabin with teal seating, subway station, massive glowing circular tunnel, concrete debris.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Woman stands on a wet street surrounded by cars and glowing signs, one vertical sign reading "HZN NO". Cut to man sitting on a teal bench in a curved cabin, red skies visible through porthole windows.
4-8s — Man shifts gaze right. Cut to a rapid forward POV flight through a dense city canyon of glowing neon skyscrapers.
8-12s — Woman stands confidently on a station platform, hair blowing as a train blurs past her left. Cut to man standing in a heavily neon-lit outdoor station with poster-covered columns.
12-15s — Tracking shot from behind the man and woman as they stand side-by-side, facing a massive, glowing, multi-colored circular tunnel structure surrounded by scattered rubble.

CAMERA
Eye-level mid shots, rapid forward motion POV, fast-paced rhythmic editing. Shallow depth of field during character portraits, wide expansive angles for environments.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High-contrast neon illumination, glowing atmospheric haze, wet ground reflections. Highly saturated red, cyan, vibrant teal, bright orange, deep black shadows.

AUDIO
Heavy cyberpunk synthwave music track, ambient rain splashing, rushing train whoosh, low atmospheric electric hum.

AVOID
Photorealism, 3D CGI rendering, muted colors, pastel palettes, static cinematography, plain backgrounds, illegible floating text.
