## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 3D animated anime fantasy

SCENE
Chibi anime character wearing a brown capybara costume, a green pineapple crown, and a yellow-and-green sports jersey reading "10". Sprawling, vibrant green grassy field beneath a bright blue sky filled with fluffy white clouds. Giant pink cherry blossoms.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Character faces a rolling hill from behind. A massive, sudden explosion of bright pink petals erupts directly from the grass, framed as a wide scenic shot.
4-8s — The floating petals magically swirl together to form a giant, translucent whale shape. The character turns to the camera and runs laterally across the meadow.
8-12s — Character surfs atop the giant flying petal whale against the sky. Cuts to a tight mid-shot of her bouncing joyfully on a soft mound of pink petals.
12-15s — Character sits laughing happily in a petal pile as blossoms rain down. Camera tracks backward as she sprints forward, trailing magical pink petal dust.

CAMERA
Dynamic and energetic. Mix of sweeping wide tracking shots for scale and tight mid-shots for character emotion. Fast-paced cut rhythm.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Bright, high-contrast sunlight. Deep sky blue, vibrant grass green, pastel cherry blossom pink, bright yellow.

AUDIO
Wind rushing, magical whooshing during petal transformations, cheerful giggling, upbeat orchestral fantasy music.

AVOID
Photorealism, muted colors, low lighting, slow pacing, urban elements.
```
