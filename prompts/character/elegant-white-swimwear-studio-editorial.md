## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 9:16 | Style: High-fashion swimwear studio editorial

SCENE
Young Asian female model, long flowing dark wavy hair, delicate silver stud earrings, glossy pink lips. She wears a minimalist solid white two-piece string bikini with classic triangle top and tie-side bottoms. The environment is an endless, seamless bright white photographic studio backdrop.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Medium shot, framing the model from the upper thighs to the head. She smiles warmly, right hand near her chin, left hand playfully holding the side string of her bikini bottom.
4-8s — Medium-close up, framing waist-up. She drops her hands to rest gently at her hips. Her posture straightens, looking directly into the lens with a subtle, relaxed smile.
8-12s — Dynamic transition. The model sharply turns her body forward, her long dark hair whipping dramatically through the air. Her right arm raises across her chest in a fast, sweeping motion.
12-15s — Extreme close up on her face, neck, and shoulders. She gently touches her collarbone. Her expression softens into a serene, confident gaze as the camera holds tight on her glowing skin.

CAMERA
Telephoto portrait lens. Straight-on eye-level angles throughout. Slow, smooth forward push, steadily tracking from a medium framing into a tight, intimate close-up. Fluid rhythm with seamless transitions.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High-key commercial studio lighting. Strong directional softbox key light creating glowing skin highlights and clean drop shadows on her neck. Monochromatic stark white palette contrasting heavily with warm peach skin tones and deep espresso brown hair.

AUDIO
Driving, minimal deep house runway track. Faint rustling of hair and skin contact during movement.

AVOID
Cluttered backgrounds, colored wardrobe, messy hair, low-resolution artifacts, camera shake, complex scenery.
```
