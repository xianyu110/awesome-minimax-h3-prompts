## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: 3D anime, cinematic rendering, photorealistic lighting

SCENE
Anime girl with silver twin-tails, pale skin, and large blue eyes. She wears a deep burgundy velvet dress with ruffled trim, red hair ribbons, and dark boots. The background shifts between three distinct, empty environments: a vintage theatre, a modern mall, and a gothic conservatory.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Full shot. The girl stands in an empty theatre with rows of red seats, turning her head.
4-8s — Close-up profile of her face. Cut to a medium shot from behind, showing her looking out over an empty, multi-level shopping mall atrium.
8-12s — High-angle full shot inside a gothic stone greenhouse surrounded by blooming flowers. Cut to a medium shot from behind in the same location.
12-15s — Close-up of her face looking downward with a soft expression. Cut to a macro shot of her hands in ruffled sleeves delicately reaching for pink flowers.

CAMERA
Smooth, slow-paced tracking and panning. High-quality lens feel with shallow depth of field in close-ups. Pacing uses sharp, deliberate cuts between the vastly different environments.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Strong volumetric god-rays piercing through windows and dust. High contrast atmospheric light. Palette relies on deep burgundy, cool silver, warm golden sunlight, and rich natural floral tones.

AUDIO
Quiet, echoing ambient space, soft footsteps, gentle classical piano score. Light fabric rustling.

AVOID
Other characters, dialogue, fast-paced action, on-screen text, harsh artificial lighting.
```
