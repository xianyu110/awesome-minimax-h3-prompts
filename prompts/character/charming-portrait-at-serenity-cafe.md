## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cinematic photorealism, modern lifestyle portrait

SCENE
Young East Asian woman, long straight black hair, bangs, wearing a strapless pale pink floral dress. Upscale lounge and cafe featuring a coffee bar, a curved white sofa with purple and pink pillows, an illuminated pastry case, and a window reflecting interior light.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Medium close-up. The woman leans forward on the sofa, speaking directly to the camera with a bright smile. The coffee bar is softly out of focus behind her.
4-8s — Medium shot. She holds a thick open book in front of a dark window, looking up from the pages to talk directly to the lens.
8-12s — Medium shot. Back in the main lounge, leaning on the sofa. A pink neon sign reading "Serenity COFFEE & BLOOM" and a glowing neon archway illuminate the background.
12-15s — Close-up. The framing tightens as she smiles softly and continues speaking, with a lit pastry display case visible behind her.

CAMERA
Shallow depth of field, 50mm prime lens portraiture. Eye-level framing. Static camera with subtle handheld breathing. Direct subject-to-lens address. Abrupt jump cuts between distinct background angles.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Warm ambient interior lighting mixed with vibrant practical neon. Soft, flattering frontal key light on the face. Palette dominated by warm gold, magenta, pale pink, and deep plum.

AUDIO
Subtle cafe ambience, gentle lo-fi background track. Clear female spoken dialogue; lips move continuously in direct address.

AVOID
Harsh directional shadows, camera panning, extreme wide shots, distorted typography, breaking eye contact.
```
