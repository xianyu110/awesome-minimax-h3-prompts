## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cyberpunk grunge music video

SCENE
Four East Asian women. Wardrobe: graphic crop tops, black leather miniskirts, fishnet tights, charcoal cargo pants, black-and-white faux fur jacket, heavy platform boots, silver chain hardware. Setting: Industrial warehouse interior, wet concrete floor, steel staircase, large grid windows.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Group formation on reflective concrete. Two women crouch in the foreground, two stand near the metal staircase. Slow camera push-in.
4-8s — Rapid cuts. Blonde woman in faux fur stands centered; massive solid white text "MOVE" spans the background. Cut to two women executing synchronized arm choreography. Thin white crosshairs and digital HUD overlays flash on screen.
8-12s — Brunette woman in blue halter top leans toward the lens. Background features a laser grid and a floating iridescent bubble. Left-aligned white and gold text reads "STAY HUNGRY STAY FOOLISH" above a wireframe globe icon.
12-15s — Wide reverse tracking shot. The four women strut forward synchronously. A distressed white film matte borders the frame.

CAMERA
Wide lenses, medium close-ups. Smooth dolly-ins, reverse tracking walks. High-energy rhythm with hard cuts and digital UI overlay transitions.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Low-key industrial lighting mixed with practical neon tube lights resting on the floor and walls. High contrast. Palette: Deep blacks, dark greys, electric blue, vibrant magenta, stark white typography.

AUDIO
Heavy bass electronic dance track. Punchy synth beats, rhythmic digital risers.

AVOID
Natural sunlight, acoustic instruments, slow pacing, empty environments, vintage color grading.
```
