## Prompt

Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 9:16 | Style: Photorealistic selfie video

SCENE
Young woman, sleek black bob hair, tight pink cutout bodysuit with "Keor" printed on the lower hip, pink ribbed over-the-knee socks. Gray metal lounge chair. Massive packed tennis stadium background, blue hard court, active players. Legible signage: "us open", "CHASE O".

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Woman smiles directly into the lens, positioned in a seated pose, holding the camera in an extended right arm selfie setup.
4-8s — She rotates her head backward to observe the live tennis match unfolding on the court below.
8-12s — She turns her attention back to the camera lens, raising a bright neon yellow tennis ball into the frame using her left hand.
12-15s — She presents the tennis ball close to the lens, clearly displaying the exact text "MiniMax H3" printed in black, smiling warmly at the viewer.

CAMERA
Selfie perspective, wide-angle smartphone lens. Camera remains fixed relative to the subject's extended right arm. Slight handheld drift. Deep depth of field maintaining sharp focus on the subject while keeping the distant stadium crowd clearly visible.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Natural daytime sunlight, bright overhead illumination. Vivid color palette: pastel pink wardrobe, neon yellow ball, royal blue tennis court, Kelly green stadium borders, vibrant spectator crowd.

AUDIO
Continuous stadium crowd murmur, sporadic distant cheering, faint echoing thwack of a tennis racket hitting a ball.

AVOID
Camera cuts, zooming, unnatural body proportions, distorted text, artificial studio lighting.
