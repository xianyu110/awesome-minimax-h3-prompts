## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Slapstick comedy commercial

SCENE
Janitor, blue jumpsuit, green rubber boots. Mechanical toy mouse, blue body, yellow wheels, pink tail, gold wind-up key. Modern shopping mall corridor, glossy floors, digital ad screens, wooden benches, glass storefronts.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Wide shot: Janitor mops floor calmly near a digital sign reading "BUM JCCE". Floor-level tracking: Toy mouse rolls past yellow wet floor signs reading "BIONIOL".
4-8s — Low tracking shot behind mouse: Janitor chases the toy, wielding the mop like a baseball bat. He swings wildly, knocking a blue and red bucket across the glossy floor.
8-12s — Extreme close-up profile of the mechanical mouse rolling past digital ad boards. Fast cut to a low angle of the janitor swinging the mop aggressively downward near the mall's glass exit doors.
12-15s — Wide static shot: Janitor stands center frame, looking upward in exhausted defeat. The surrounding mall corridor is completely trashed with overturned benches, spilled plant pots, and scattered buckets.

CAMERA
Low-angle tracking, floor-level dynamic perspective following the toy mouse. Fast-paced action cuts during the chase. Static wide framing for the chaotic aftermath.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Bright overhead skylight illumination, clean commercial lighting. Saturated primary colors contrasting with warm neutral floors: bright blue jumpsuit, yellow signs and wheels, red and blue plastic buckets.

AUDIO
Echoing mall ambience, squeaking rubber boots, mechanical whirring of the wind-up toy, loud crashing sounds of plastic and wood, frantic slapstick orchestral chase music.

AVOID
Dark shadows, slow motion, shallow depth of field, realistic violence, unlit backgrounds.
```
