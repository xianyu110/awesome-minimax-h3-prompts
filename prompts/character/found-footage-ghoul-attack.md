## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Found footage horror, VHS glitch aesthetic

SCENE
Young Asian woman wearing a bright yellow tank top and frayed denim shorts. Emaciated ghoul with sickly pale skin, elongated limbs, sharp fangs, and bony spikes protruding from its spine. Dilapidated, pitch-black room featuring peeling green wall paint, dirt, and scattered wooden debris.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — The ghoul violently chokes the terrified woman against a deteriorating wall. She struggles desperately, her hands gripping its elongated gray arms to relieve the pressure. Framed as a medium two-shot.
4-8s — The creature unhinges its jaw, roaring directly into her face. The sheer force of the roar blows the woman's dark hair wildly backward as she screams in agony. Camera pushes in for a tight close-up.
8-12s — The brutal struggle continues. The woman frantically tries to pry the monster's unyielding grip from her throat, her expression locked in pure terror.
12-15s — Sudden harsh VHS tracking glitches distort the frame. The ghoul unexpectedly disengages, sitting back on its haunches to flash a sinister, bloody grin directly at the camera lens. The woman collapses in the background, sobbing.

CAMERA
Handheld, erratic, and shaky, simulating an amateur camcorder. Heavy use of direct flash perspective. Aggressive VHS tracking lines and analog distortion tear through the final seconds.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Harsh, direct frontal flash lighting creating stark, pitch-black background shadows. Color palette dominated by sickly corpse grays, stark neon yellow, faded denim blue, and murky browns.

AUDIO
Shrill, panicked female screaming, wet guttural monster roaring, frantic physical struggling. Piercing tape static and audio tearing during the final glitch sequence.

AVOID
Smooth camera stabilization, professional three-point lighting, cheerful atmosphere, modern clean lenses, shallow depth of field.
```
