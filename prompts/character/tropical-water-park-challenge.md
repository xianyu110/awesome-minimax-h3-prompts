## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Photorealistic sunny action commercial

SCENE
Young Asian woman with long dark hair wearing a white two-piece bikini. A tropical outdoor water park resort featuring tall palm trees, white canopy tents, and a tall blue and yellow spiral water slide. A course of colorful inflatable stepping pads spans a bright blue pool.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Wide shot establishing the obstacle course. The woman leaps from a raised platform onto green and yellow floating pads. A large red inflatable in the foreground clearly reads "GOAL".
4-8s — Camera pushes in quickly. Symmetrical water jets erupt from black pipes along the pool edges, creating a dynamic spray archway. The woman balances on a yellow pad.
8-12s — Medium shot. She navigates the water arch, laughing with arms extended for balance as the heavy spray hits her face and torso.
12-15s — She steps onto the red "GOAL" pad, loses her footing, and plunges into the bright blue water, leaving a large white splash ring.

CAMERA
Smooth continuous dolly forward tracking the subject's run. Starts wide to establish the park geometry, transitioning to a medium action shot focused on her reactions. 

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Bright midday sunlight casting hard shadows. High contrast and highly saturated primary colors emphasizing the vivid blue pool, yellow inflatables, and the bright white bikini.

AUDIO
Energetic summer instrumental pop. Ambient rushing water, sudden pressurized hissing from the archway jets, wet footsteps, a heavy splash impact, and joyful laughter.

AVOID
Overcast skies, slow motion, motion blur, distorted text, missing water reflections.
```
