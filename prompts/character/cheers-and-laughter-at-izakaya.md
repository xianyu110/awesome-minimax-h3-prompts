## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Photorealistic lifestyle vlog

SCENE
Two young East Asian women at a wooden table in a Japanese izakaya. Left woman wears a navy blue silk slip dress; right woman wears a cream silk slip dress. Both hold large glass mugs of foaming draft beer. Background features wooden paneled walls, paper menus with vertical calligraphy, a large red crab wall decoration, and softly blurred diners.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Both women look directly at the lens, smiling warmly. They raise their large beer mugs and clink them together in a celebratory toast, framed waist-up.
4-8s — They simultaneously take a long drink from their mugs, obscuring their lower faces. As they lower the glasses, a thick mustache of white beer foam remains on the right woman's upper lip.
8-12s — The left woman notices the foam, playfully points her index finger at her friend's face, and both women break into genuine, wide-mouthed laughter.
12-15s — The laughter subsides into bright smiles as they look back to the lens. They push their mugs forward for a final cheers, while the right woman playfully sticks her tongue out.

CAMERA
Static medium close-up. Eye-level, fixed viewpoint mimicking a tripod-mounted vlog camera. Zero cuts, single continuous take.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Warm, inviting interior lighting from glowing overhead spherical pendants. Amber hues of the beer and rich brown wood tones contrast with the sleek navy and cream garments.

AUDIO
Bustling restaurant ambience, sharp glass clink, drinking sounds, loud joyful laughter, warm acoustic background track.

AVOID
Cuts, camera movement, zooming, serious expressions, daylight, empty glasses.
```
