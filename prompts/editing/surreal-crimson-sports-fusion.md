## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 9:16 | Style: High-energy, surreal sports montage

SCENE
Multiple athletes including a female soccer player in a green tracksuit, a male golfer in a red jacket, and a shirtless male basketball player in red shorts, situated in abstract, dark, and dramatically lit environments.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — A woman with braids in a green and yellow jacket stares intensely, then walks forward in a dark stadium. A white soccer cleat kicks a yellow and red ball.
4-8s — A massive soccer ball dominates the foreground. Rapid cuts reveal a golfer mid-swing, extreme close-ups of a gloved hand gripping a club, and the textured dimples of a golf ball.
8-12s — A golfer wearing yellow sunglasses and a white cap reading "SUPER" swings, spraying debris. The golf ball flies into a pitch-black void.
12-15s — A shirtless basketball player raises a ball against a twilight sky, then leaps into the air with glowing red motion trails. The ball falls onto grass before the scene distorts into a melting, abstract red sphere.

CAMERA
Kinetic pacing with rapid cuts. Mix of extreme macro close-ups, low-angle hero shots, and dramatic slow-motion action freezes.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High-contrast, directional lighting with intense crimson red gels and deep shadows. Primary palette features emerald green, vivid red, stark white, and deep teal.

AUDIO
Heavy synthetic bass, aggressive swooshes, sharp impact sounds of sporting equipment, and distorted low-frequency hums.

AVOID
Muted colors, flat lighting, continuous long takes, realistic physics, mundane settings.
```
