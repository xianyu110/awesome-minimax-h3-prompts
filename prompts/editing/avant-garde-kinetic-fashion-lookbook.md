## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: High-fashion editorial lookbook with bold kinetic typography

SCENE
Female model with sleek hair wearing avant-garde structural fashion, including oversized blazers, metallic armor, and sharp-shouldered dresses. Backgrounds alternate between concrete brutalism, glowing metallic tunnels, and minimalist photo studios. Giant text overlays integrate with the environment.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Model in an oversized black suit poses against concrete pillars and a red industrial corridor. Framed center. Floating text reads: "DON'T BLINK" and "BREAK THE RULES".
4-8s — Model struts through a red metallic tunnel in a white and silver futuristic corset, then poses in a stark split-tone studio. Framed medium-wide. Text reads: "NO APOLOGIE" and "MAKE THEM".
8-12s — Model wears a reflective silver top against a dark glitch backdrop, then walks strictly in profile in a white and silver suit across a seamless white void. Text reads: "6411", "LOUDER LOUDER LOUDER", and a halftone "NO".
12-15s — Model poses in a sharp-shouldered emerald green outfit in front of a torn-poster wall, then stands motionless within a dismantled photo studio setup. Wide framing. Text reads: "WE DON'T MISS", "4:0:21.18.1 1:2:3:8", "OWN THE NIGHT", and "UNSTOPPABLE".

CAMERA
Locked-off center framing, strict linear tracking, rapid rhythmic smash cuts.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High-contrast studio flash, harsh directional spotlights. Dominant palette: stark white, deep black, metallic silver, crimson red, emerald green.

AUDIO
Heavy electronic bass beat, rhythmic camera shutter clicks, sharp metallic swooshes.

AVOID
Soft focus, warm natural sunlight, organic environments, slow crossfades, shaky handheld camera.
```
