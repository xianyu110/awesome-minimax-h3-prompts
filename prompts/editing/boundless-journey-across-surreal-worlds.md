## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cinematic surreal environment transition montage

SCENE
Young woman, dark wavy hair, white satin dress with a high slit, camel blazer draped over shoulders, emerald and diamond necklace. She travels seamlessly through rapidly changing fantastical environments.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Woman walks confidently through a rainy cyberpunk city with glowing neon billboards and flying vehicles, transitioning instantly to a traditional Japanese temple with falling pink cherry blossoms.
4-8s — The environment shifts to an underwater realm featuring large sharks, glowing jellyfish, and massive crystal formations. The scene cuts to a gray lunar base with astronauts; the woman turns away, facing a giant Earth in the sky.
8-12s — Seen from a profile angle, she stands in a foggy mystical forest surrounded by giant bioluminescent mushrooms. The backdrop abruptly changes to a dark volcanic landscape with actively erupting lava and smoke.
12-15s — She faces forward inside a modern minimalist penthouse with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking a sea of clouds at sunset. The final cut places her in deep space against a brightly lit spiral galaxy and stars.

CAMERA
Locked-on tracking shot, maintaining medium-full framing. Rapid, seamless smash cuts between backgrounds while the subject's position remains relatively stationary.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Highly dynamic and contrasting. Neon magentas and cyans, warm golden hour tones, deep aquatic blues, fiery orange lava, and vivid cosmic purples. 

AUDIO
Driving electronic beat. Heavy swooshing transition sound effects synchronized with the rapid environmental cuts. No dialogue.

AVOID
Wardrobe changes, character morphing, camera shake, shallow depth of field obscuring backgrounds, muted colors.
```
