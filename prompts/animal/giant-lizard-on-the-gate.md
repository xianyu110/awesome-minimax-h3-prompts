## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Realistic everyday vlog video

SCENE
Three young Asian women wearing casual summer clothes including tank tops, t-shirts, and denim shorts, standing outside a suburban house. A large grey monitor lizard clinging to a black metal driveway gate, and a residential street with houses.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Medium shot of three women standing by an open residential gate, laughing and reacting as a large lizard climbs the metal bars. One woman records the animal on a smartphone while a motorcycle handlebar and rearview mirror frame the foreground.
4-8s — Wide shot of a sunny suburban street as a delivery rider wearing a turquoise helmet drives a scooter toward the camera, carrying a cardboard parcel and a square delivery box.
8-12s — Medium side-profile shot of the three women watching the lizard on the gate. The woman in the green shirt holds her smartphone close to film the reptile as it clings to the vertical bars.
12-15s — Low-angle shot looking down at the women's bare legs and feet wearing sandals and sneakers on the bright concrete driveway near the black metal gate.

CAMERA
Handheld digital camera with natural depth of field. Cuts between eye-level medium framing, a wide street view, and a downward low-angle perspective.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Bright midday outdoor sunlight casting crisp shadows on concrete. Naturalistic colour palette featuring foliage greens, neutral greys, white, and matte black metal.

AUDIO
Ambient outdoor street atmosphere, faint scooter engine hum, and casual background laughter from the women.

AVOID
Unrealistic CGI effects, studio lighting, cinematic color grading, distorted anatomy, camera shake, on-screen text overlays.
```
