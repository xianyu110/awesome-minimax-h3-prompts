## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: Cinematic drama, emotional cross-cutting dialogue

SCENE
A distressed woman with an updo wearing a beige turtleneck sweater and holding a dark coat in an airport terminal gate at night. A somber man in a grey t-shirt inside a dimly lit kitchen with rain-speckled windows.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Medium shot of the woman standing among rows of empty blue airport seats, speaking anxiously on her phone.
4-8s — Cut to a medium shot of the man leaning against a kitchen counter, listening solemnly on his phone.
8-12s — Cut to a tight close-up of the woman, teary-eyed and speaking with deep emotion.
12-15s — Cut to a tight close-up of the man closing his eyes with a pained, heartbroken expression.

CAMERA
50mm and 85mm prime lens feel, shallow depth of field, static framing, eye-level medium shots cutting cleanly to intimate close-ups.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Airport setting uses cool white overhead lighting, dark glass reflections, deep blues, and beige. Kitchen setting features warm pendant lighting, heavy shadows, and amber outdoor bokeh.

AUDIO
Low airport terminal hum, soft ambient drone, distant rain against window glass, quiet emotional phone dialogue.

AVOID
Camera shake, daylight, fast whip pans, exaggerated body motion, upbeat background music.
```
