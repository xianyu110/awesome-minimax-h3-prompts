## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 21:9 | Style: Cinematic fashion eyewear commercial, photorealistic

SCENE
Young man, dark wavy hair, beard, gradient aviator sunglasses, navy blazer, white collared shirt. Young woman, long brown hair, copper silk wrap dress, gold hoop earrings. Modern urban street, paved road, glass-facade office buildings.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Man walks confidently down the center of a city street toward the camera. Framed as a medium tracking shot.
4-8s — Woman joins him, walking on his left. They turn their heads to look at each other and smile warmly.
8-12s — Extreme close-up on the man's face. The city skyline reflects in his sunglasses, followed by a clear reflection of the approaching woman. He smiles.
12-15s — Medium close-up of the woman playfully adjusting the man's sunglasses. Cut to the man looking off-camera, then directly into the lens. Overlay text fades in: "See the world in style 😎".

CAMERA
Smooth backward Steadicam tracking. Shallow depth of field blurring the architectural background. Sharp focus on faces and eyewear reflections. Fast, deliberate commercial editing rhythm.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Golden hour backlighting, prominent warm lens flares hitting the lens edge. Soft, flattering fill light on the subjects' faces. Color palette features deep navy, crisp white, rich copper, amber sunlight, and cool blue-grey architectural reflections.

AUDIO
Upbeat, sophisticated lifestyle electronic background music. Subtle ambient city breeze and soft footsteps. No dialogue.

AVOID
Mismatched reflections, distorted sunglasses frames, extra fingers during the adjustment, misspelled text, harsh shadows, unnatural walking gait.
```
