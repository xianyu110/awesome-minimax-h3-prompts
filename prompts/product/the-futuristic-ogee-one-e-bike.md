## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: High-energy kinetic product commercial

SCENE
Glossy red futuristic electric bicycle featuring a continuous curved frame, black components, and belt drivetrain. Minimalist architectural white grid background transitioning to a solid white studio sweep.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Black kinetic typography flashes on a white grid, reading "THE". Vertical text slides rapidly. Close-up of the red frame crossing the grid alongside "90 KM". Fast motion-blurred pan of the entire bike moving horizontally.
4-8s — Extreme macro shot of a glossy red tube with "BELT DRIVE" text. Crisp, static close-ups detail the black rear hub's toothed belt mechanism and a drilled silver disc brake rotor.
8-12s — Close-up profile of matte black handlebars and brake levers against the grid. Cut to a static full side profile of the swooping red bike. The camera smoothly orbits to a 3/4 rear perspective.
12-15s — The bike rests in a full side profile against a pure white, shadowless backdrop. Bold black text appears in sequence overhead: "OGEE", followed by "OGEE ONE", and finally "NO STRAIGHT LINES."

CAMERA
Fast-paced commercial videography. Aggressive whip pans, rapid staccato cuts, extreme macro detailing, and smooth orbital sweeps. Locked-off static frames for text reveals.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High-key studio lighting. Soft diffused boxes create sharp linear reflections on the glossy frame. High-contrast palette: Metallic cherry red, matte black, stark white, polished silver.

AUDIO
Driving electronic bass track. Fast whoosh effects for text animations, subtle mechanical freewheel ticking, synthetic motor hum during motion shots.

AVOID
Human riders, outdoor settings, traditional metal bike chains, straight frame geometry, cluttered environments, lifestyle elements.
```
