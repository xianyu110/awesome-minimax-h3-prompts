```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: High-end fashion commercial

SCENE
Elegant woman in a monochromatic pink tailored suit, pink stilettos, and sunglasses. Pink vintage car. Pale pink leather handbag featuring a distinct gold geometric clasp. Minimalist white modernist architecture situated on a palm-lined desert highway.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Wide tracking shot of the pink vintage car driving toward a white building. Text overlay: "GOLDEN HOUR". Cut to a tight close-up of a pink stiletto stepping onto the asphalt.
4-8s — Medium profile shot of the woman standing confidently near the car. Cut to a close-up of her hand smoothly opening the car trunk.
8-12s — Product shot of the pink handbag resting inside the trunk. Text overlay: "LEATHER & SILHOUETE". Quick cut to an extreme macro detail of the gold clasp.
12-15s — Over-the-shoulder close-up of the woman turning to look at the camera, holding the bag. Text overlay: "CARRY YOUR GRACE". Hard cut to a black screen with white text: "FREE & ROMANTIC" above "LIVE FREELY, LOVE DEEPLY".

CAMERA
Sleek, stabilized tracking, medium fashion portraits, and extreme macro inserts. Smooth, deliberate pacing with rhythmic cuts. Prominent shallow depth of field on all close-ups.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Warm golden hour sunlight casting long, dramatic shadows. Soft, diffused ambient fill. Color palette dominated by pastel pinks, rose gold, crisp architectural white, and warm muted desert tans.

AUDIO
Ambient warm wind, crisp heel clicks on pavement, smooth metallic latch opening. Polished, elegant downtempo lounge music. No dialogue.

AVOID
Cluttered backgrounds, harsh fluorescent lighting, fast handheld camera shaking, distorted typography, mismatched color schemes.
```
