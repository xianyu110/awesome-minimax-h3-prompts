## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: High-end beauty commercial

SCENE
Young Asian woman, long wavy brown hair, cream silk tie-neck blouse, gold hoop earrings, delicate gold pendant necklace. Upscale bathroom interior featuring gray-veined marble countertops, dual oval mirrors with thin gold frames, brushed gold fixtures, and subtle greenery.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Medium shot of the woman smiling warmly at the camera, holding a closed black and gold mascara tube. She looks down to twist the cap open.
4-8s — Framed closer, she lifts the wand, examining it while looking off-camera. She raises it to her eye and begins applying mascara to her right upper eyelashes.
8-12s — Extreme close-up isolating her eye and the wand coating her lashes. Cuts to her smiling softly at her reflection in the mirror, wand in hand.
12-15s — Macro product shot. The black and gold tube stands vertically on the marble counter, displaying gold text "IINIIOVIMI". Flanked by blooming white roses and blurred skincare jars.

CAMERA
Locked-off tripod shots with smooth, deliberate cuts. Pronounced shallow depth of field in all close-ups and the final product shot. High definition macro focus used for facial details.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Soft, diffused natural daylight illuminating the subject evenly. Bright, airy atmosphere. Color palette highlights cream, brushed gold, deep black, pristine white, and warm peach skin tones.

AUDIO
Uplifting, serene instrumental background music. Crisp foley sounds of twisting the plastic cap, faint brushing, and the final placement of the product on marble. No dialogue.

AVOID
Handheld camera shake, harsh shadows, cluttered backgrounds, unnatural eye tracking, dull skin tones.
```
