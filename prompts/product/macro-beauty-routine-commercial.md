## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: High-end cosmetics commercial, photorealistic

SCENE
East Asian woman, long dark hair, white silk robe. Marble vanity countertop, illuminated ring mirror, arranged gold and rose-gold cosmetic bottles, bright white room background.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Medium shot of woman holding a frosted bottle and glass dropper. Cuts to close-up of her dispensing a drop onto her cheek, then macro shot of clear liquid touching the skin.
4-8s — Extreme close-up of a beige teardrop sponge blending foundation on her cheek, followed by a fluffy rose-gold brush applying blush.
8-12s — Extreme close-up of a pink doe-foot applicator gliding glossy tint onto her lips.
12-15s — Medium close-up framing her face and shoulders. She showcases glowing makeup, looking at the camera with a gentle smile, hand gracefully positioned.

CAMERA
Macro beauty lenses, shallow depth of field. Static camera positioning with fast, rhythmic cuts between wide setting shots and extreme detail inserts.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Soft, diffused studio lighting. High-key exposure with ring light reflections in the eyes. Crisp whites, marble grays, metallic golds, and glossy pastel pinks.

AUDIO
Pristine room tone, distinct liquid droplet sound, soft sponge tapping, light brush sweeping, serene lo-fi background music.

AVOID
Harsh shadows, cluttered background, camera movement, motion blur, skin blemishes.
```
