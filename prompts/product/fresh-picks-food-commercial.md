## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 9:16 | Style: Dynamic studio food commercial

SCENE
Assorted styled food items (loaded fries, tomato mozzarella toast, strawberry waffle, yogurt bowl, pastel ice cream, pasta salad) on geometric, diagonally color-blocked surfaces.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Opens with a flat-lay wide shot of six dishes arranged on cream, navy, and green diagonal blocks. Cuts to a macro shot of steaming loaded fries as red seasoning falls onto white sauce.
4-8s — Cuts to a close-up of tomato and cheese toast in a black baking pan. Fresh basil leaves fall onto the toast against a diagonal red and cream background.
8-12s — Close-up of a waffle as syrup pours over fresh strawberries against a pink background with bokeh. Transitions to a yogurt and fruit bowl with floating cereal and blueberries as honey streams down.
12-15s — Rainbow sprinkles fall onto a pastel ice cream cone topped with whipped cream and a cherry. Concludes with a flat-lay of five dishes on blue, pink, and white diagonal blocks. Legible text reads "FRESH PICKS" at the top and "made for today" at the bottom right.

CAMERA
Locked-off static framing. Mix of top-down flat lays and eye-level macro close-ups. Fast-paced rhythmic cuts matching the falling ingredients.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Bright, hard studio lighting creating sharp, directional drop shadows. Vibrant, high-contrast palette utilizing bold color-blocked backgrounds (navy, pastel pink, powder blue, cherry red, cream).

AUDIO
Snappy, upbeat commercial pop music. Foley includes sizzling, thick liquid pouring, and the light clattering of falling herbs, cereal, and sprinkles.

AVOID
Messy plating, soft lighting, muted colors, camera panning, handheld movement, cluttered backgrounds.
```
