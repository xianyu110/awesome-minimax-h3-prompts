## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: High-end luxury fashion commercial

SCENE
Red-haired female model, black leather jacket, pink metallic corset, black leather mini skirt, black knee-high boots, "PRADA" necklace. Triangular glass perfume bottle with black cap containing pink liquid, labeled "PRADA MILANO DAL 1913". Glossy black corridor, vertical pink neon light strips, floor fog.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Extreme close-up of the perfume bottle on a reflective surface. Cut to the woman walking confidently down the neon-lit corridor.
4-8s — Close-up of the woman holding the bottle forward, focusing on the glass. Rack focus to her face as she holds the bottle near her cheek.
8-12s — Wide full-body shot of the woman posing in the foggy corridor. She raises the bottle to her shoulder level.
12-15s — Medium shot prioritizing the woman's face with the bottle blurred in the foreground. Rack focus shifting sharply to the bottle on the table while the woman blurs in the background.

CAMERA
Shallow depth of field, frequent rack focusing between foreground product and background model. Smooth tracking shots, slow push-ins. Rapid commercial-style cuts. High gloss, sharp focus.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High-contrast dark studio lighting. Dominant vertical pink neon practical lights. Glossy black floor reflections. Color palette: Neon pink, deep black, metallic silver, pale skin tones, vibrant copper-red hair.

AUDIO
Heavy synth-bass electronic beat, rhythmic clicking heels, subtle atmospheric hum of neon tubes.

AVOID
Daylight, natural settings, flat lighting, cluttered backgrounds, casual streetwear.
```
