## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Style: High-end beauty commercial, photorealistic

SCENE
Black woman, natural hair updo, white plush bathrobe. Luxury bathroom setting, marble vanity, large mirrors, chrome sink fixtures, white flowers, bright window with outside greenery.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Close-up on a white Dove soap bar and box resting on wet marble beside white flowers. The word "PURE" is visibly traced into the mirror condensation in the background.
4-8s — Medium shot. The woman lathers the soap in her hands over the chrome sink. Bold white 3D text "SOFTNESS" hovers directly above the counter.
8-12s — Close-up profile. The woman massages thick white soap foam onto her face. Floating 3D text "CARE" tracks with her soapy cheek. She then admires her clean face in the mirror as the text "REFRESH" appears on the glass.
12-15s — Medium close-up. She smiles brightly at the camera, holding the Dove box beside her face. Large overlaid graphic text reads: "Dove SOFTNESS YOU CAN FEEL. CARE YOU CAN TRUST."

CAMERA
Smooth slow push-ins. Shallow depth of field on macro product shots. Gentle, evenly paced commercial editing.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
Soft diffused daylight, warm mirror backlighting. Bright, clean palette of pure whites, marble greys, rich brown skin tones, and navy blue accents.

AUDIO
Gentle water splashing, thick soap lathering friction. Soothing, upscale ambient spa instrumental music.

AVOID
Harsh shadows, cluttered countertops, deformed floating text, unnatural foam textures, rapid camera movements.
```
