## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 9:16 | Style: High-end cosmetics commercial, photorealistic beauty photography.

SCENE
Female model with slicked-back ponytail. Oversized dusty rose suit, matching bandeau, gold hoop earrings. "MOIST HEIST LIP OIL" bottle featuring a silver cap and magenta liquid. Settings include a minimalist white runway and abstract liquid or smoke environments.

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Full shot of model walking a white runway. Cuts to medium shot holding the product, then macro shot of hands pulling the applicator wand from the "MOIST HEIST LIP OIL" bottle.
4-8s — Extreme close-up of glossy lips. Cuts to product surrounded by floating water droplets, then a macro face shot emphasizing glossy lips and skin texture.
8-12s — Product rests on rippling liquid silver with pink metallic bubbles. Cuts to front-facing close-up of model applying the lip oil. Product stands amid billowing pink smoke.
12-15s — Bottle held beside the model's eye. Extreme close-up of parted, highly reflective lips. Final product shot with floating pink droplets and text: "STEAL THE SHINE. KEEP THE MOISTURE."

CAMERA
Macro lenses for tight beauty shots. Fast-paced, rhythmic commercial cuts. Shallow depth of field on facial close-ups. Smooth, slow pans across abstract product environments. 

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High-key studio lighting with softbox diffusion. Intense, crisp specular highlights on lips, liquid, and glass. Palette dominated by dusty rose, magenta, metallic silver, and stark white.

AUDIO
Upbeat, driving electronic runway music. Foley includes sharp glass bottle clicks, subtle liquid swishes, and high-heeled footsteps. No dialogue.

AVOID
Matte finishes, cluttered backgrounds, unkempt hair, muted colors, flat lighting.
```
