## Prompt

```text
Duration: 15 seconds | Aspect ratio: 9:16 | Style: Clean UGC beauty tutorial and product demonstration.

SCENE
Young woman, icy blonde hair, tanned skin, manicured nails with white tips, plain white t-shirt. Bathroom setting, round wall mirror, white vanity, black and white soap dispensers, double glass wall sconces, white paneled door. A black makeup tube with white text reading "NOIRLIFT".

SHOT BREAKDOWN
0-4s — Close-up. Woman faces the camera, speaking actively while displaying the "NOIRLIFT" tube near her cheek.
4-8s — Medium shot via mirror reflection. Over-the-shoulder framing of the woman unscrewing the wand, then applying mascara to her left eyelashes.
8-12s — Extreme close-up on the woman's face. Heavy diagonal sunlight casts sharp shadows. She smiles, blinks, and displays her elongated eyelashes while speaking.
12-15s — Close-up. Woman applies mascara again under strong sunlight, then returns to holding the "NOIRLIFT" tube up, smiling and speaking excitedly to the viewer.

CAMERA
Smartphone camera feel, steady handheld. Eye-level angles. Fast-paced rhythmic cuts between standard close-ups, mirror reflections, and extreme close-up facial shots.

LIGHTING & PALETTE
High-key natural daylight blended with warm ambient sconce lighting. Later shots feature harsh, sharp diagonal sunlight across the face. Palette emphasizes warm skin tones, pale blonde hair, stark whites, deep blacks, and soft pink lips.

AUDIO
Upbeat acoustic pop music. Subtle plastic friction sounds of the mascara wand unscrewing. Clear spoken dialogue throughout as the subject's lips move frequently.

AVOID
Text morphing, inconsistent nail polish, distorted mirror reflections, unnatural eyelash clumping, warped background geometry.
```
